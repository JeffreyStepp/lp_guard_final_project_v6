HOW IT WORKS

From observation to decision

This school project keeps the original LP Guard idea recognizable while deliberately leaving transaction execution out of scope.

01

Observe the market

Request live price, 24-hour price change, volume, and liquidity from DEX Screener.

02

Define the range

Choose lower and upper boundaries around the current ETH price and enter the capital being planned.

03

Measure exposure

LP Guard calculates range status and estimates the value split between ETH and the selected stablecoin.

04

Keep the user in control

The result is information for a human decision. This website does not connect a wallet, sign, swap, mint, or rebalance.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

A small front-end view of a much larger LP Guard concept.

The larger LP Guard project separates operating responsibilities so that market observation, accounting, analysis, user decisions, verification, and wallet execution do not collapse into one system. This JavaScript version intentionally focuses on only two safe pieces: market observation and range analysis.

LedgerPreserves facts and capital history.
RiskMeasures exposure without transaction authority.
OptimizeCompares alternatives without executing them.
GuardVerifies intended operations before movement.
